# CSR Adapter — Operational Checklist

*Strategic context: [hetexecs/funding-adapters/csr](https://nsreehari-code.github.io/hetexecs/funding-adapters/index.html)*

---

## Pre-Batch (additional to standard)

- [ ] MOU signed with sponsor (scope, KPIs, reporting cadence)
- [ ] Safeguarding policy acknowledged
- [ ] Sponsor reporting template confirmed
- [ ] Baseline data fields aligned with sponsor KPIs
- [ ] Brand guidelines confirmed (logo placement, messaging)

## During Batch (additional to standard)

- [ ] Sponsor updates sent at agreed cadence (weekly/fortnightly)
- [ ] Photo evidence captured per sponsor requirements
- [ ] Safeguarding protocols followed (consent, data protection)
- [ ] Sample evidence collected for mid-term report

## Closure (additional to standard)

- [ ] Sponsor interim report submitted
- [ ] Success stories captured (3–5 with photos)
- [ ] Before/after comparison data compiled

## Impact Report (Day 90)

- [ ] Full impact report drafted
- [ ] Quantitative data: trained, completed, certified, income change
- [ ] Qualitative data: testimonials, case studies, photos
- [ ] Sustainability narrative: what continues after the grant
- [ ] Report submitted to sponsor

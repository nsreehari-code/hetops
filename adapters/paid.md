# Paid Training Adapter — Operational Checklist

*Strategic context: [hetexecs/funding-adapters/paid](https://nsreehari-code.github.io/hetexecs/funding-adapters/index.html)*

---

## Pre-Batch (additional to standard)

- [ ] Pricing finalized (fee structure, installment options)
- [ ] Refund policy published
- [ ] Payment collection method set up (UPI, bank transfer, online payment)
- [ ] Scholarship slots defined (if any)
- [ ] Marketing materials created (flyer, WhatsApp message, social posts)

## During Batch (additional to standard)

- [ ] Payment status tracked per learner
- [ ] Satisfaction check-in at Week 2 (early warning for drop-off)
- [ ] Value front-loading ensured (tangible outcomes in first 2 weeks)
- [ ] NPS / satisfaction pulse at Week 4

## Closure (additional to standard)

- [ ] Completion certificates issued (your brand)
- [ ] NPS survey collected
- [ ] Testimonial capture (for marketing)
- [ ] Alumni club / WhatsApp group activated
- [ ] Upsell offered (advanced module, mentorship, peer group)

## Growth Engine (Post-Batch)

- [ ] Referral program activated
- [ ] Repeat purchase tracking (did learners return for another course?)
- [ ] Testimonials published (website, WhatsApp status, social media)
- [ ] Case studies created for future marketing

BL-1: Pre-Authorization

Prevent opening a batch you'll lose money on. This is where most TPs fail.

Core Question

"If I open this batch today, can I survive till Tranche-3?"

Decision Checklist

  • ☐ Scheme eligibility confirmed (PMKVY / SSDM / NULM / CSR / Paid)
  • ☐ Center readiness verified (branding, biometric, internet)
  • ☐ Trainer validity checked (ToT active, SSC certified)
  • ☐ District approval / DSC comfort note obtained
  • ☐ Cash flow projection done (can self-finance until Tranche-1)

Golden Rule

If Pre-Auth fails → Do not open the batch.

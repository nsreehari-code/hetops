BL-4: Training Delivery

Survive audits and avoid attendance disputes. Delivery is compliance-critical, not pedagogical.

Core Question

"Can I defend every class conducted?"

Execution Checklist

  • ☐ Daily attendance captured (biometric + fallback)
  • ☐ Trainer substitution protocol followed (if needed)
  • ☐ Equipment usage verified
  • ☐ Weekly internal review completed
  • ☐ Week 2 QA checkpoint: attendance + artifact review
  • ☐ Week 4 QA checkpoint: digital presence verified
  • ☐ Week 6 QA checkpoint: mock assessment completed

Mindset

Delivery is compliance-critical, not pedagogical. An un-defended class = audit risk.

Owner

Trainer (primary delivery) · Center Coordinator (guardrail)

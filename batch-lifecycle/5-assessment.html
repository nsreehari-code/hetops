BL-5: Assessment Readiness

Protect 70% of funding. This is risk management, not academics.

Core Question

"Who is likely to fail, and why?"

Execution Checklist

  • ☐ Mock assessment completed (Week 6 — non-negotiable)
  • ☐ Remedial action log created for at-risk candidates
  • ☐ Assessment agency coordinated (dates, logistics)
  • ☐ Candidate readiness declaration signed
  • ☐ All candidate documents assessment-ready

Key Principle

Every failed candidate is lost revenue, not just a low score.

Owner

Assessment Lead

BL-7: Livelihood & Tranche-3

Turn training into money. This pack directly determines profitability. If documentation lags, Tranche-3 dies.

Core Question

"Can I prove income?"

Evidence Checklist

  • ☐ Udyam certificate obtained/verified
  • ☐ Digital storefront evidence captured (WhatsApp catalog screenshot)
  • ☐ Income proxy collected (UPI transactions, order threads, bank extract)
  • ☐ Geo-tagged photos taken (market stall, product display)
  • ☐ 30-day follow-up completed
  • ☐ 60-day follow-up completed
  • ☐ 90-day follow-up completed
  • ☐ Success story / testimonial captured

Income Proxy Acceptance Criteria

  • UPI transaction screenshots (even ₹500 counts)
  • WhatsApp order conversation threads
  • Market stall photos with visible price tags
  • Udyam registration confirmation
  • Bank statement showing business transactions

Follow-Up Cadence

DayVerify
30Catalog still active, at least 1 transaction
60Orders/month, repeat customers
90Success story + peer mentoring + referral

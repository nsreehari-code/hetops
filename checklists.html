BL-1: Pre-Authorization

Owner: Program Manager

Decision (Go / No-Go)

  • Is the scheme eligible for this course? (PMKVY / SSDM / NULM / CSR / Paid)
  • Can we self-finance until Tranche-1 arrives?
  • Is there a credible mobilization pipeline for this geography?

Execution

  • Center geo-tagged and registered on SID (if govt)
  • Center branding complete (Skill India banners, photos taken)
  • Biometric devices available and tested
  • Internet connection stable
  • Equipment mapped to job role available
  • Trainer assigned with valid ToT certification
  • District Skill Committee contacted / comfort note obtained

Evidence

  • Center photos (exterior, classroom, branding) archived
  • Trainer ToT certificate copy filed
  • Scheme eligibility confirmation documented
  • Cash flow projection saved

BL-2: Mobilization & Enrollment

Owner: Program Manager + Center Coordinator

Decision

  • Does this candidate meet scheme eligibility criteria?
  • Does this candidate have a pre-existing enterprise? (for entrepreneur courses)
  • Can this candidate realistically pass assessment?

Execution

  • Aadhaar copy collected and verified
  • Bank account verified (in candidate's name)
  • Mobile number linked to Aadhaar
  • Aadhaar–mobile–bank linkage confirmed
  • Age eligibility verified
  • Consent & declaration forms signed
  • Enterprise pre-existence validated (photos, product samples)
  • Portal enrollment completed (if govt)

Evidence

  • Aadhaar copy filed
  • Bank passbook / cancelled cheque copy filed
  • Consent forms filed
  • Enterprise pre-existence photos archived
  • Portal enrollment screenshot saved

BL-3: Batch Kick-Off

Owner: Program Manager + Center Coordinator

Execution

  • Day-1 ceremony conducted
  • Branding photos taken (classroom, banner, group photo)
  • Biometric dry-run completed (all devices working, all candidates enrolled)
  • Trainer-learner mapping documented
  • Batch activation confirmed on SID (if govt)
  • Baseline capture completed (income range, current channels, pricing habits)
  • WhatsApp group created
  • Code of conduct shared and acknowledged
  • Tools check done (smartphones, UPI, printer/scanner)

Evidence

  • Day-1 ceremony photos archived (geo-tagged)
  • Biometric test log saved
  • Baseline survey responses filed
  • SID batch activation screenshot saved

BL-4: Training Delivery

Owner: Trainer + Center Coordinator

Daily

  • Biometric attendance captured (+ manual fallback if needed)
  • Session conducted as per curriculum plan
  • Learner artifacts produced (per weekly plan)

Weekly

  • Attendance rate reviewed (flag if < 80%)
  • Equipment usage verified
  • Internal review note written
  • Documentation Officer: evidence folder up to date

QA Checkpoints

  • Week 2: Attendance trend acceptable · First artifact complete per learner · Drop-off risk identified → intervention
  • Week 4: Digital presence verified (catalog live?) · Trainer substitution log · Equipment issues escalated
  • Week 6: Mock assessment conducted · Remediation list created · Go/no-go decided per candidate

Trainer Substitution Protocol

  • Backup trainer pre-approved
  • Handover notes prepared
  • Learners informed
  • Attendance continues uninterrupted

BL-5: Assessment Readiness

Owner: Assessment Lead

Decision

  • Mock pass rate ≥ 70%? (If not, escalate)
  • All candidates have complete document files?
  • Assessment agency confirmed dates and logistics?

Execution

  • Mock assessment conducted (Week 6 — non-negotiable)
  • Mock results documented per candidate
  • Remedial action plan created for at-risk candidates
  • Extra coaching sessions scheduled (if needed)
  • Assessment agency coordination complete (dates, logistics, assessor travel)
  • Candidate readiness declaration signed
  • All candidate photos, IDs, and artifacts ready for assessor

Evidence

  • Mock assessment scores filed
  • Remedial action log filed
  • Assessment agency confirmation email/letter saved
  • Candidate readiness declarations filed

BL-6: Certification & Closure

Owner: Documentation Officer + Program Manager

Execution

  • Assessment completed (all candidates assessed)
  • Assessment results received from agency
  • Certificates issued to all passing candidates
  • Certificate receipt acknowledgment collected
  • Final attendance reconciliation completed (portal vs actual)
  • Batch closure completed on portal (if govt)
  • Feedback survey collected from all learners
  • Testimonials captured (minimum 3–5, written or video)
  • All physical documents filed and organized
  • Digital evidence archive complete and backed up
  • Sponsor/CSR interim report submitted (if applicable)

Evidence

  • Assessment result sheet filed
  • Certificate distribution register filed
  • Final attendance register reconciled and filed
  • Portal closure screenshot saved
  • Feedback survey responses compiled
  • Testimonial files (text/video) archived

BL-7: Livelihood & Tranche-3

Owner: Documentation Officer

Day 30

  • Follow-up call completed for each learner
  • WhatsApp catalog still active? (screenshot)
  • At least 1 sales transaction or inquiry? (evidence captured)
  • Any learner needing support identified → intervention

Day 60

  • Follow-up call completed
  • Income proxy collected (orders/month, UPI transactions, repeat customers)
  • Udyam certificate obtained (or registration in progress)
  • Geo-tagged photos taken (if applicable)

Day 90

  • Final follow-up completed
  • Success story / testimonial captured (compelling, specific)
  • Peer mentoring connection made (connecting strong learners to new cohort)
  • Referral captured (for next batch)
  • Income evidence compiled into proof pack
  • Tranche-3 submission prepared (if govt)
  • Impact report submitted (if CSR)
  • CCF Section I (Iteration Notes) filled

Evidence Pack (Final)

  • Udyam certificates
  • Digital storefront screenshots
  • Income proxy documents (UPI, bank, order threads)
  • Geo-tagged photos
  • Testimonials / success stories
  • Before/after income comparison

Funding Adapter Checklists

Additional items on top of the standard checklists above, depending on funding model.

Government (PMKVY / SSDM / DDU-GKY / NULM)

Pre-Batch

  • TP registration active on SID
  • Training Center (TC) geo-tagged and verified on SID
  • Trainer mapped to job role on SID with valid ToT
  • Scheme eligibility confirmed (PMKVY / SSDM / DDU-GKY / NULM)
  • Batch created on SID (any error blocks payment)

During Batch

  • Aadhaar biometric attendance captured daily
  • Backup biometric device available
  • Attendance fallback protocol documented (if internet/device fails)
  • SID attendance sync verified weekly
  • Skill India branding visible in all class photos

Assessment

  • Third-party assessment agency engaged
  • Assessment scheduled via SSC/NSDC channel
  • Assessor travel and logistics confirmed
  • Assessment result uploaded to portal

Closure

  • Batch closed on SID portal
  • Certificate issued via SSC
  • Final attendance reconciliation: portal vs physical register

Tranche Submissions

  • Tranche 1 (Enrollment): Batch enrolled on SID · All learner details uploaded · Center verified
  • Tranche 2 (Assessment): Assessment completed · Results uploaded · Certificates issued · Batch closed
  • Tranche 3 (Livelihood): Livelihood evidence compiled (Udyam, income proxy, geo-tagged photos) · Evidence uploaded · Self-declaration by learners

CSR

Pre-Batch

  • MOU signed with sponsor (scope, KPIs, reporting cadence)
  • Safeguarding policy acknowledged
  • Sponsor reporting template confirmed
  • Baseline data fields aligned with sponsor KPIs
  • Brand guidelines confirmed (logo placement, messaging)

During Batch

  • Sponsor updates sent at agreed cadence (weekly/fortnightly)
  • Photo evidence captured per sponsor requirements
  • Safeguarding protocols followed (consent, data protection)
  • Sample evidence collected for mid-term report

Closure

  • Sponsor interim report submitted
  • Success stories captured (3–5 with photos)
  • Before/after comparison data compiled

Impact Report (Day 90)

  • Full impact report drafted
  • Quantitative data: trained, completed, certified, income change
  • Qualitative data: testimonials, case studies, photos
  • Sustainability narrative: what continues after the grant
  • Report submitted to sponsor

Paid Training

Pre-Batch

  • Pricing finalized (fee structure, installment options)
  • Refund policy published
  • Payment collection method set up (UPI, bank transfer, online)
  • Scholarship slots defined (if any)
  • Marketing materials created (flyer, WhatsApp message, social posts)

During Batch

  • Payment status tracked per learner
  • Satisfaction check-in at Week 2 (early warning for drop-off)
  • Value front-loading ensured (tangible outcomes in first 2 weeks)
  • NPS / satisfaction pulse at Week 4

Closure

  • Completion certificates issued (your brand)
  • NPS survey collected
  • Testimonial capture (for marketing)
  • Alumni club / WhatsApp group activated
  • Upsell offered (advanced module, mentorship, peer group)

Growth Engine (Post-Batch)

  • Referral program activated
  • Repeat purchase tracking (did learners return?)
  • Testimonials published (website, WhatsApp status, social media)
  • Case studies created for future marketing

7 micro-packs — one per batch lifecycle stage. Your ops pack is 80% checklists, 20% SOPs. Checklists enforce whether; SOPs explain how.

Each micro-pack contains: 1–2 decision checklists (go/no-go), 1 execution checklist, 1 evidence checklist, and a clear owner.

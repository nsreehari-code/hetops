# Evidence Spine — WMDG (Women's Micro-Entrepreneurship & Digital Growth)

**Blueprint Version:** WMDG-v1 (8 weeks, 3 sessions/week)

---

## Baseline (Day 1)

| Artifact | How captured | Format |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| Current monthly income range | Survey / interview | Form response |
| Current sales channels | Survey | Form response |
| Current pricing method | Survey | Form response |
| Existing product photos | Learner provides | Photos |
| Enterprise pre-existence proof | Photos, samples | Photos + notes |

---

## Participation (Weeks 1–8)

| Week | Artifact | How captured |
|------|----------|-------------|
| 1 | Product photo + differentiator sentence | Learner creates, trainer reviews |
| 2 | Pricing sheet + break-even calculation | Workbook exercise |
| 3 | Quick reply templates + review plan | WhatsApp setup |
| 4 | WhatsApp catalog live + Maps listing + UPI QR | Screenshots |
| 5 | 12-post content pack | Created in class |
| 6 | Sales scripts + mock assessment result | Practical exercise |
| 7 | Udyam registration (or awareness certificate) | Portal screenshot |
| 8 | Capstone pitch deck / presentation | Live presentation |

---

## Competency Proof (Week 6–8)

| Artifact | How captured | Assessment type |
|----------|-------------|----------------|
| Mock assessment score | Trainer-administered | Internal |
| Capstone presentation | Panel evaluation | Internal |
| Live WhatsApp catalog | Screenshot verification | Practical |
| Sales role-play score | Observed exercise | Internal |
| Third-party assessment result (if govt) | Assessment agency | External |

HET Skilling OS — Batch Lifecycle

The repeating unit of skilling. Every batch goes through these 7 stages. Course design is already done — this is where execution happens.

Thumbrule: If you're doing, it belongs here. If you're deciding, it belongs in hetexecs.
This repo is the field manual — checklists, templates, runbooks. Not strategy, not live data.

The 7 Stages

Each stage has a micro-pack: decision checklist, execution checklist, evidence checklist, clear owner.

BL-1Pre-Auth BL-2Mobilize BL-3Kick-Off BL-4Delivery BL-5Assessment BL-6Certification BL-7Livelihood
BL-1

Pre-Authorization

Prevent opening a batch you'll lose money on. Scheme eligibility, center readiness, trainer validity, district approval.

"If I open this batch today, can I survive till Tranche-3?"
BL-2

Mobilization & Enrollment

Kill enrollment-time rejections. Learner eligibility, document verification, enterprise pre-existence validation.

"Will these candidates pass portal + assessment + livelihood proof?"
BL-3

Batch Kick-Off

Lock Tranche-1 payment. Day-1 ceremony, biometric dry-run, trainer-learner mapping, batch activation on SID.

"Can an auditor reconstruct Day 1 exactly?"
BL-4

Training Delivery

Survive audits and avoid attendance disputes. Daily attendance SOP, trainer substitution, equipment verification.

"Can I defend every class conducted?"
BL-5

Assessment Readiness

Protect 70% of funding. Mock assessments, remedial action, assessment agency coordination.

"Who is likely to fail, and why?"
BL-6

Certification & Closure

Close the loop cleanly. Certificate receipt, final reconciliation, batch closure on portal, document archival.

"Is anything left unuploaded or unverified?"
BL-7

Livelihood & Tranche-3

Turn training into money. Udyam certificates, digital storefront evidence, income proxies, geo-tagged photos.

"Can I prove income?"

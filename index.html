Execution Stages (Owned Here)

Stage 4

Package

Marketing, pricing/funding, enrollment funnel. Mobilization through SHGs, district channels, or direct-to-consumer.

"Do I have the right learners, the right funding, and the right calendar?"
Stage 5

Launch

Batch setup, onboarding, baseline capture, portal activation. This is where Tranche-1 gets locked.

"Can an auditor reconstruct Day 1 exactly?"
Stage 6

Deliver

Live training, attendance, QA checkpoints, mid-course correction. Delivery is compliance-critical, not pedagogical.

"Can I defend every class conducted?"
Stage 7

Certify / Close

Assessment, certificates, archive, batch closure. Sloppy closure = delayed Tranche-2.

"Is anything left unuploaded or unverified?"
Stage 8

Outcome Track

30/60/90 follow-up, livelihood evidence, alumni loop. This pack directly determines profitability.

"Can I prove income?"

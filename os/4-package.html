Stage 4: Package

Marketing, pricing/funding, enrollment funnel. The seam between strategy and execution.

Core Question

"Do I have the right learners, the right funding, and the right calendar?"

What Happens Here

  • Marketing — flyers, WhatsApp messages, SHG outreach
  • Pricing / funding model locked (Govt/CSR/Paid or mixed)
  • Enrollment funnel designed and activated
  • Batch calendar set
  • Partner MOUs signed (if applicable)

What You Create

  • CCF Section D (Curriculum detail — filled now)
  • Flyer + WhatsApp invite message
  • Enrollment form (paper + digital)
  • Batch calendar

Actors

ActorRole
Program ManagerEnrollment funnel, batch calendar, pricing/funding
Mobilization / OutreachSHG engagement, WhatsApp campaigns, district coordination
CEOFunding model decision, partner MOUs
Govt: District Skill Committee / SSDMTarget allocation, scheme eligibility
CSR: SponsorKPI agreement, grant terms

Mobilization Truth

Mobilize pre-qualified learners — women already earning something. Existing SHGs, artisans, home businesses.

PMKVY does not pay you to "discover talent." It pays to formalize and upgrade income.

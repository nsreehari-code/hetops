Roles & Ownership

Who owns what, at each stage. An ops pack dies if ownership is unclear.

Role Definitions

RoleResponsibilityPrimary Stages
Program ManagerEnd-to-end batch ownership. Go/no-go decisions. Escalation point.BL-1, BL-2, BL-3, oversight of all
Center CoordinatorCenter operations. Infrastructure, logistics, daily management.BL-2, BL-3, BL-4
TrainerDelivery. Attendance, artifacts, learner engagement.BL-4 (primary)
Assessment LeadAssessment coordination. Mock assessments, agency liaison.BL-5
Documentation OfficerEvidence capture, archival, follow-up. The compliance spine.BL-5, BL-6, BL-7

Critical Principles

  • Trainers should never be sole custodians of compliance. Documentation Officer is a role, not a task.
  • Program Manager owns the go/no-go. If Pre-Auth fails, they stop the batch.
  • Documentation Officer is the lifeline at BL-7. Most TPs die at livelihood tracking because nobody owns the follow-up.

Design for the Worst Day

Assume:

  • Trainer absent
  • Internet down
  • Assessor late
  • Candidate documentation incomplete

Your system should still hold. Every role should have a known backup.
